SMS Consent Process

Drink Specials SMS Notification System — how employees opt in

This program is operated by The Last Resort Beach Bar, LLC for internal use by our own staff and managers only. It is not open to the public, and there is no public sign-up form. This page is publicly viewable with no login required, and is the complete record of how consent is collected — nothing about verifying this process requires access to any internal system.

Who is enrolled

Only current bartenders and managers of The Last Resort Beach Bar. Enrollment happens in person, as part of onboarding. After an employee verbally agrees using the exact script below, an owner records their name and number in a private internal admin tool — that tool is for internal record-keeping only, is intentionally not public (it manages real staff contact info and a live payment system), and is not where consent happens. Consent happens verbally, in person, using the script documented on this public page.

The exact consent script

Before adding an employee's number, an owner reads them this script in person:

"I'd like to add your phone number to our internal drink-specials and shift-coverage system. You'll get a text whenever a bartender submits a special for approval, a text if an open shift needs covering, plus one daily sales summary text. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving these texts. Do you agree to receive these texts at this number?"

Only after the employee verbally agrees does the owner enter their name and phone number into the internal admin tool. No number is ever added without this step. Employees who consented before this shift-coverage feature existed are asked to re-confirm consent the next time they sign in to the internal app, so no one receives a new message type they didn't originally agree to.

What messages they receive

  • A text when a bartender submits a drink special, asking the manager to approve it.
  • A confirmation text once a special is approved.
  • A text if an open shift needs covering, and a confirmation once someone picks it up.
  • One daily sales and discount summary text.

Opt-out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. See our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for full details.
