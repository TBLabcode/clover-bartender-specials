Drink Specials SMS Notification System

This SMS program is operated by The Last Resort Beach Bar, LLC ("we," "us") for internal use by our staff and managers to submit and approve drink specials.

What this program does

  • Bartenders submit a proposed drink special through an internal web form.
  • Managers receive a text message asking them to approve or ignore the request.
  • Managers may receive a daily sales summary text.

Message frequency varies based on staff activity — typically a few messages per day. Message and data rates may apply.

Consent

By participating in this program as a staff member, you consent to receive SMS messages related to drink special approvals and daily sales summaries at the phone number you provide. This program is for internal staff use only and is not open to the general public.

Opt-out

Reply STOP, CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, REVOKE, STOPALL, or OPTOUT at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply START to resubscribe.

Help

Reply HELP or INFO at any time for assistance, or contact us using the information below.
