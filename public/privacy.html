Privacy Policy

Drink Specials SMS Notification System

This policy explains how The Last Resort Beach Bar, LLC ("we," "us") handles information collected through our internal drink-specials SMS notification system.

What we collect

  • Staff name, as entered when submitting a special.
  • Manager phone numbers, used to send and receive approval messages.
  • Message content and timestamps related to special requests and approvals.

How we use it

Information is used solely to operate this internal staff tool — routing special requests for approval, applying approved prices in our point-of-sale system, and sending related confirmations. We do not use this information for marketing.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in/consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. The only third party involved is our SMS delivery platform (Twilio), which processes messages solely to deliver them on our behalf and does not use this data for its own marketing purposes.

Data retention

Approved and pending special requests are retained only as long as needed to operate this tool. You may request removal of your information by contacting us below.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts from this program at any time.
