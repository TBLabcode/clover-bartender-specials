SMS Consent Process

Shift-Coverage SMS Notification System — how employees opt in

This program is operated by {{legalName}} for internal use by our own staff and managers only. It is not open to the public, and there is no public sign-up form. This page is publicly viewable with no login required, and is the complete record of how consent is collected — nothing about verifying this process requires access to any internal system.

Who is enrolled

Only current bartenders and managers of {{businessName}}. Enrollment happens in person, as part of onboarding. After an employee verbally agrees using the exact script below, an owner records their name and number in a private internal admin tool — that tool is for internal record-keeping only, is intentionally not public (it manages real staff contact info), and is not where consent happens. Consent happens verbally, in person, using the script documented on this public page.

The exact consent script

Before adding an employee's number to this system, an owner reads them the following script in person:

"We're adding your phone number to {{businessName}}'s shift-coverage texts. You'll get a text if an open shift needs covering, and a confirmation once someone picks it up. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving these texts. Do you agree to receive these texts at this number?"

Only after the employee verbally agrees does the owner enter their name and phone number into the internal admin tool. No number is ever added without this step.

What messages they receive

  • A text if an open shift needs covering, describing the date and shift and how to claim it by replying.
  • A confirmation text once someone picks the shift up.

Message frequency varies with staff activity — typically only a few messages per month. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for assistance. See our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for full details.
