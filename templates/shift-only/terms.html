Shift-Coverage SMS Notification System

This SMS program is operated by {{legalName}} ("we," "us") for internal use by our staff and managers to arrange coverage for open shifts.

What this program does

  • An owner or manager can request that an open shift be covered.
  • Bartenders receive a text describing the open shift and can claim it by replying.
  • Everyone involved gets a confirmation text once someone picks the shift up.

Message frequency varies based on staff activity — typically only a few messages per month. Message and data rates may apply.
