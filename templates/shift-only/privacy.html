Shift-Coverage SMS Notification System

This policy explains how {{legalName}} ("we," "us") handles information collected through our internal shift-coverage SMS notification system.

What we collect

  • Staff names and phone numbers, used to send and receive shift-coverage messages.
  • The shift details an owner or manager submits, and which bartender replies to claim a shift.
  • Message content and timestamps related to shift-coverage requests.

How we use it

Information is used solely to operate this internal staff tool — coordinating shift coverage requests among staff and sending related confirmations. We do not use this information for marketing.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in/consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. The only third party involved is our SMS delivery platform (Twilio), which processes messages solely to deliver them on our behalf and does not use this data for its own marketing purposes.

Data retention

Shift-coverage requests are retained only as long as needed to operate this tool. You may request removal of your information by contacting us below.
