SMS Consent Process

Drink Specials SMS Notification System — how employees opt in

This program is operated by {{legalName}} for internal use by our own staff and managers only. It is not open to the public, and there is no public sign-up form. This page is publicly viewable with no login required, and is the complete record of how consent is collected — nothing about verifying this process requires access to any internal system.

Who is enrolled

Only current bartenders and managers of {{businessName}}. Enrollment happens in person, as part of onboarding. After an employee verbally agrees using the exact script below, an owner records their name and number in a private internal admin tool — that tool is for internal record-keeping only, is intentionally not public (it manages real staff contact info and a live payment system), and is not where consent happens. Consent happens verbally, in person, using the script documented on this public page.

The exact consent scripts

This system uses two separate phone numbers for two separate message types. Before adding an employee's number to either one, an owner reads them the applicable script in person. Agreeing to one number's script does not enroll an employee in the other — each is a separate opt-in.

Specials-approval number (drink-special approvals and the daily sales summary):

"I'd like to add your phone number to our internal drink-specials system. You'll get a text whenever a bartender submits a special for approval, plus one daily sales summary text. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving these texts. Do you agree to receive these texts at this number?"

Shift-coverage number (a second, separate number used only for shift requests):

"We're adding a second number for shift-coverage texts — separate from the specials-approval number you're already signed up for. You'll get a text if an open shift needs covering, and a confirmation once someone picks it up. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving these texts. Do you agree to receive these texts at this number?"

Only after the employee verbally agrees to a given script does the owner enter their name and phone number for that number into the internal admin tool. No number is ever added without this step. Employees already enrolled in the specials-approval number before shift-coverage existed are asked to complete the shift-coverage opt-in separately, the next time they sign in to the internal app, rather than assuming it's already covered.

What messages they receive

  • Specials-approval number: a text when a bartender submits a drink special, asking the manager to approve it; a confirmation once it's approved; one daily sales and discount summary text.
  • Shift-coverage number: a text if an open shift needs covering, and a confirmation once someone picks it up.

Opt-out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. See our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for full details.
